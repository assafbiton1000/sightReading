Delete Your Account and Data

App: FreePace Piano: Sight Reading (com.freepacepiano.app) · Last updated: July 26, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your FreePace Piano account and the data associated with it. Creating an account in FreePace Piano is optional — if you have only ever used the app as a guest, no account data about you exists on our servers at all.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open FreePace Piano and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app or your account, email assafbiton@gmail.com from the email address registered to the account, with the subject "FreePace Piano account deletion request". We will verify ownership of the address and delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by reply once it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account record — email address, display name, hashed password, sign-in providerPermanently deleted
Profile — rank, badge, supporter statusPermanently deleted
Leaderboard entry — display name and pointsPermanently deleted and removed from the public leaderboard
Cloud backup of your practice statistics, points and session historyPermanently deleted
Forum posts and comments you wrotePermanently deleted

What is not deleted

DataWhy / how to remove it
Practice history, streaks, statistics and settings stored locally on your deviceThis data never leaves your device and is not part of your account. Remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings.
Purchase and payment records for any donation you madeHeld by Google Play, not by us. Google retains these for legal, tax and accounting purposes under Google's own policies; we cannot delete them on your behalf.

Deleting only part of your data (without deleting your account)

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove specific data:

  • Forum posts and comments — open the Community forum, select your own post or comment, and tap Delete. Only the author sees this control, and deletion is permanent.
  • Anything else — email assafbiton@gmail.com from your registered address describing which data you want removed (for example your leaderboard entry or your cloud-backed practice statistics). We will action it within 30 days and keep the rest of your account intact.

Retention

Deletion removes your data from our live database immediately. Encrypted infrastructure backups held by our hosting provider are rotated on a rolling basis and any residual copies are purged within 30 days. We keep no other copies of your account data after deletion.
