Privacy Policy for FreePace Piano

Last updated: July 26, 2026

FreePace Piano ("the app", "we", "our") is a piano sight-reading practice app. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Microphone Access

The app requests microphone access to detect the pitch of notes you play on a real piano, so it can check your practice in real time. This audio is analyzed entirely on your device using a local pitch-detection algorithm running inside the app. Raw audio is never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere — it exists only in memory for the fraction of a second needed to detect a pitch, and is then discarded. We (and no third party) ever receive or have access to this audio.

If you prefer not to grant microphone access, the app offers an on-screen piano keyboard as an alternative input method that requires no microphone at all.

2. Account Information (optional)

Creating an account is optional — the app is fully usable as a guest. If you choose to sign up (by email/password or "Continue with Google"), we collect and store the following on our backend infrastructure (provided by Supabase):

  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Your account creation date
  • Your password, which is hashed and never stored or visible in plain text (email/password sign-up only)

This information is used solely to authenticate you and let you access your account. We do not use your email for marketing and do not share it with advertisers.

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile information with our authentication provider under Google's own privacy policy; we do not receive your Google password.

3. Practice Data

Your practice history, streaks, accuracy statistics, points and app settings (difficulty level, sound, language, dark mode, etc.) are always stored locally on your device.

If you are signed in to an account, a copy of your points and practice session history is also backed up to our backend so that it survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. This backup is private to you: our database rules allow only your own account to read or write it. If you use the app as a guest, nothing is uploaded and your practice data stays on your device only.

Your app settings and your daily reminder preference are never uploaded.

4. Leaderboard and Community Forum (optional, publicly visible)

If you are signed in, two features publish content to other users of the app:

  • Leaderboard — your display name and points score are visible to other signed-in users of the app. Your email address is never shown.
  • Community forum — posts and comments you write are stored on our backend and shown to other users alongside your display name. Posts are reviewed by a moderator before publication. You can permanently delete your own posts and comments at any time from within the forum.

Do not post personal information you do not want to be public. If you never use these features, nothing of yours appears in them.

5. Advertising

The app includes an optional rewarded advertisement on the "Support" screen, provided by Google AdMob. It is never shown automatically — it plays only if you explicitly choose to watch it in order to support the app.

To serve ads, the AdMob SDK collects device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data, and transmits them to Google. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms. You can limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. We do not receive your identifiers and do not combine ad data with your account.

6. Voluntary Donations (in-app purchases)

The app lets you make an optional donation to support development, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address or any other payment details — Google handles the transaction. Our backend receives only the purchase token issued by Google, which it verifies against the Google Play Developer API in order to confirm the donation is genuine and grant the supporter badge on your profile. Records of the transaction itself are held by Google Play under Google's own policies.

7. Notifications

If you enable the daily practice reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device using your device's notification system. No data about your reminder preferences leaves your device.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your account details, practice data and forum content are not sold, rented or shared for marketing purposes. The third parties involved in the app are:

  • Supabase — our hosting provider, which securely stores the optional account system, cloud practice backup, leaderboard and forum described above, acting on our instructions.
  • Google — for AdMob advertising (section 5), Google Play Billing (section 6), and, only if you choose "Continue with Google", for sign-in (section 2).

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. This immediately and permanently removes your account record (email, name, password), your profile, your leaderboard entry, your cloud practice backup and your forum posts from our backend. It does not affect practice data already stored locally on your device, which you can remove by uninstalling the app.

You can also delete individual forum posts without deleting your account. Full instructions, including how to request deletion by email if you can no longer access the app, are on our account deletion page.

10. Children's Privacy

FreePace Piano is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Account creation is optional and requires an email address.

11. Security

Account data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored using industry-standard encryption provided by our backend infrastructure provider (Supabase). Access to your private data is enforced at the database level by row-level security rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
